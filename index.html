About Me

Hello! This is Korina, an environment 3D artist who's worked in Diablo II Resurrected, Apex Legends, and SIMS Mobile. She has 6 years of experience creating realistic props and architectural pieces for AAA games. She is skilled in organic modeling, creating assets within the constraints of the game engine or technical specifications, and has a good understanding of the PBR metallic workflow. She is a gamer at heart, and is currently into Divinity 2 and Darkwood.
